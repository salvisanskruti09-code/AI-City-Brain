# AI City Brain: Urban Edge Cell

### One Local Area. Multiple Problems. One Intelligent Brain.

## About the Project

AI City Brain is a student innovation concept for a hyperlocal AI-powered urban decision-support system.

It aims to explore how different local signals can be combined to identify potential risks related to traffic congestion, water anomalies, and garbage overflow.

## The Problem

Urban problems are often monitored separately. However, traffic, water, garbage, weather, and local activity can influence one another within a small area.

Our concept focuses on understanding these connections to support proactive decision-making.

## Proposed Solution

The system follows four stages:

**DETECT → PREDICT → SUGGEST → HUMAN ACTION**

Google Gemini is proposed as the contextual reasoning layer that analyzes aggregated information and generates recommendations for human operators.

## Nine Hyperlocal Signals

1. Crowd Level
2. Vehicle Count
3. Auto Availability
4. Water Level
5. Water Flow
6. Garbage Fill Level
7. Tank Level
8. Weather
9. Local Timetable / Activity Schedule

## Key Features

- Traffic congestion risk analysis
- Water anomaly and tank overflow risk identification
- Garbage overflow risk monitoring
- Cross-domain risk analysis through the proposed Ripple Engine
- Human-supervised recommendations
